## 0.32.1 — Vegetation occlusion hotfix
The previous approximate polygons and per-fragment body depth caused discontinuous silhouettes. The natural scene now uses a conservative green-pixel mask restricted to manually calibrated foreground regions, at 1024×576, with a coherent depth decision per fish and a side latch while projected body/fin proxies contact foliage. Steering and bounded depth correction keep the selected side. The mask is rebuilt from the loaded photo and synchronized to WebGL and Canvas Worker; classic and close-up rendering remain unaffected. Old approximate wood/stone/substrate occlusion is temporarily removed; existing collision proxies are retained. This is not full 3D plant geometry and color segmentation is conservative. Visual acceptance, real GPU and CI remain required.


## 0.32.2 — Natural Occlusion & Smooth Depth
User accepted architecture 0.32 on 25/11/2026; 0.32.1 not accepted due to ragged leaf edges and abrupt size changes. Removed both direct obstacle-induced depth corrections. Depth integration now limited to 0.13 units/s, independent of frame rate; leaf steering is gradual and the side decision persists through proximity and a short clear interval. Coverage uses morphological closing/opening, bounded enclosed-hole filling, edge feathering and bilinear sampling in both renderers; opacity is independent of the fish's physical depth. Existing approximate photo regions are not exact plant geometry. Six pure synthetic checks passed; browser, actual photographic contour, GPU, Docker and biological regression remain pending. Do not merge PR #4 until the patch is accepted.
